Our Coffee Products

At Aroma Coffee & Tea House, we serve delicious coffee prepared to suit different tastes and preferences. Our coffee products are perfect for starting your morning, enjoying a break, or relaxing with friends.

1. Espresso

Espresso is a rich, strong coffee made by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee beans. It is ideal for customers who enjoy a bold flavour and concentrated coffee experience.

2. Cappuccino

Cappuccino is a delicious coffee drink made with espresso, steamed milk, and a layer of milk foam. It offers a smooth, creamy taste and is perfect for coffee lovers who enjoy a balanced flavour.

3. Caffè Latte

Our caffè latte combines espresso with steamed milk and a light layer of foam. It has a mild, creamy flavour that makes it a wonderful choice for customers who prefer a less intense coffee drink.

Our Tea Products

Our tea selection offers comforting and refreshing beverages for every occasion. Whether you prefer traditional tea or soothing herbal blends, we have options to help you enjoy a relaxing moment.

1. Black Tea

Black tea is a classic beverage with a rich flavour and refreshing aroma. It can be enjoyed plain or with milk and sugar, making it a popular choice for breakfast or an afternoon break.

2. Green Tea

Green tea has a light, fresh taste and a delicate aroma. It is a great option for customers who prefer a gentle tea flavour and a simple, refreshing drink.

3. Herbal Tea

Herbal tea is prepared using herbs, flowers, or other plant ingredients. It comes in a variety of flavours, such as peppermint and chamomile, offering a comforting drink for customers who enjoy aromatic herbal blends.
